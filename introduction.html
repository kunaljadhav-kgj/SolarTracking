Introduction

A Solar Tracking System is a technology that adjusts a solar panel's position according to the direction of sunlight. It helps the panel receive more direct sunlight as the sun moves across the sky.

Traditional solar panels remain in a fixed position, so the angle of sunlight changes throughout the day. A tracking system helps address this by turning the panel toward the sun.

How this project works

Light Dependent Resistors (LDRs) detect light intensity from different directions. A controller processes the sensor readings and signals a motor to adjust the panel's position.

Main Objectives

  • Improve the collection of solar energy.
  • Track the direction of sunlight automatically.
  • Reduce energy loss caused by unsuitable panel positioning.
  • Minimize the need for manual adjustment.
  • Promote renewable and sustainable energy.
  • Develop an automatic, cost-effective tracking system.

Types of Solar Trackers

  • Single-axis: Moves the panel along one axis, generally from east to west.
  • Dual-axis: Moves the panel along two axes to follow sunlight in horizontal and vertical directions.
Project goal: Use sunlight more effectively by adjusting the solar panel's direction automatically.
