Advantages & Future Scope

Advantages

  1. Increased energy collection: The panel can receive more direct sunlight by following the sun.
  2. Improved panel performance: Tracking can improve energy collection compared with a fixed panel under suitable conditions.
  3. Automatic operation: Sensors and a controller adjust the panel without continuous manual effort.
  4. Renewable energy use: Solar power is a clean, renewable energy source.
  5. Reduced manual work: The panel does not need to be repositioned by hand throughout the day.
  6. Technology integration: The system can be extended with Arduino, IoT and other smart technologies.
  7. Environment friendly: Solar electricity generation produces no direct greenhouse-gas emissions during operation.

Future Scope

  1. Use AI to predict sunlight direction and optimize panel movement.
  2. Add IoT monitoring so performance can be checked remotely.
  3. Use weather sensors to respond to clouds, rain and other conditions.
  4. Integrate automatic panel cleaning to reduce dust build-up.
  5. Connect smart batteries and energy-management systems.
  6. Adapt the design for solar power plants, agriculture and other large-scale applications.
  7. Use GPS and astronomical calculations to estimate the sun's position from location and time.
Note: Actual energy improvement depends on sunlight, panel type, weather and the tracking system's design.
