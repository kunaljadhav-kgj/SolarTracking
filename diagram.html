Circuit Diagram

This block diagram shows the main signal flow in an Arduino-based solar tracking system.

☀ Sunlight↓ LDR Sensor System↓ Arduino UNO↓ Servo Motor↓ Solar Panel Movement↓ Solar Energy Output

Main Components

  • Arduino UNO: Reads sensor values and controls the motor.
  • LDR sensors: Detect light intensity from different directions.
  • Servo motor: Adjusts the solar panel position.
  • Resistors: Form voltage-divider circuits with the LDRs.
  • Solar panel, breadboard and wires: Provide energy conversion and circuit connections.

Example Connections

  • LDR divider 1 midpoint → Arduino A0
  • LDR divider 2 midpoint → Arduino A1
  • Servo signal wire → Arduino D9
  • Connect Arduino ground and the servo supply ground together.
Important: Use a suitable external 5V supply for servo motors when needed. Do not connect a solar panel directly to Arduino input pins; use appropriate power-management circuitry.
